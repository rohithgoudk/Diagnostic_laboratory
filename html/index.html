Inside our lab

Precision you can see for yourself

01

Fully automated analysers

Barcoded tubes go straight onto chemistry, haematology and immunoassay analysers. No hand-written labels, no mix-ups.

  • Up to 1,800 tests an hour
  • Results flow straight to your report
02

Quality checked every morning

Control samples run before any patient sample. If a single control drifts, testing stops until it is fixed.

  • Daily internal QC with Westgard rules
  • Monthly external proficiency testing
03

Reviewed by a pathologist

Every report is reviewed and signed by a senior pathologist. Critical values are phoned to your doctor within 30 minutes.

  • MD pathologists on every shift
  • Second opinion on unusual results
