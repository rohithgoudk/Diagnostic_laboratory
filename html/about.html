Our story

A small lab that refused to cut corners

STACKLY Diagnostics began in a two-room clinic on Omalur Road with one pathologist, one technician and a second-hand microscope. Patients told us the same thing again and again: reports came late, nobody explained them, and prices changed depending on who you asked.

So we built the lab we wanted for our own families — fixed prices, reports the same day, and a pathologist who would pick up the phone. Fourteen years later we run 18 centres, but every report is still signed by a doctor.

“Behind every sample is someone waiting anxiously for an answer. Our job is to make that wait short, and that answer right.”

Dr. Meera IyerFounder & Lab Director

Milestones

Fourteen years, one step at a time

  1. 2012

    Doors open

    A two-room lab on Omalur Road with one pathologist and 40 patients in the first week.

  2. 2014

    First automated analyser

    Same-day sugar and lipid reports become standard for every patient.

  3. 2015

    Home collection launched

    Two phlebotomists on scooters start visiting homes across Salem town.

  4. 2017

    Microbiology wing

    Cultures and antibiotic sensitivity testing move in-house.

  5. 2019

    NABL accreditation

    Every department accredited to ISO 15189 after a year of audits.

  6. 2021

    Molecular lab

    A clean-room RT-PCR suite opens, running 3,000 tests a day at its peak.

  7. 2023

    18 centres

    Collection centres reach Attur, Omalur, Mettur and Edappadi.

  8. 2026

    2 million reports

    And still every one of them signed by a pathologist.
